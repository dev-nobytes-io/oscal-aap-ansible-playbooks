# Authored control catalogs

OSCAL catalogs for obligations that have **no authoritative OSCAL publication**:

- `pspf.json` — Protective Security Policy Framework
- `privacy-app.json` — Australian Privacy Principles (Privacy Act 1988)
- `soci-cirmp.json` — SOCI Act / CIRMP Rules obligations

These are this project's reading of published obligations. They are **not**
official, not endorsed, and not legal advice. Every control carries a citation
link to the authoritative source text so a reviewer can check the reading.

These catalogs model **obligations**, not host checks. Most of their content is
organisational and cannot be tested by Ansible at all. They earn their place by
crosswalking to the ISM controls that can produce supporting evidence — see
`../mappings/`.
